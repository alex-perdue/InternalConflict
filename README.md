## THE POLITICS OF INTERNAL CONFLICT

These are course materials for my course on contentious politics at the national and subnational levels entitled The Politics of Internal Conflict. They were originally created for PLSC 389U, taught through in spring 2027 at Binghamton University.  As these are my first lecture slides and this is the first lecture course I am likely to repeat, I wanted to put my materials here. However, you, reader who is not alex-perdue, are more than welcome to take some/all of this, provided you attribute it to me.

## Contents

- [`syllabus/`](syllabus/): LaTeX Source and PDF
  - [`ICSyllabus.pdf`](syllabus/ICSyllabus.pdf): Current version of the PDF as presented to my students.
  - [`ICSyllabus.tex`](syllabus/ICSyllabus.tex): Current version of the .tex file that, when compiled, generates the PDF as presented to my students.
  - [`ICSyllabus.bib`](syllabus/ICSyllabus.bib): Current version of the .bib file that, when paired with the .tex file, should compile to the references as they are presented in the PDF.

## Important Notices
* All LaTeX Beamer slides and the syllabus were compiled using Overleaf, using the pdfLaTeX compiler and the 2026 TeX Live implementation.
* Where there is a .bib file or images in the subfolder, you will need to download the whole subfolder for the file to compile correctly.
* All .sty files were generated with the assistance of Claude Sonnet 5/5.5 from a header/styling template for Beamer presentation I have used for years.
* All of the stylings were generated by Claude Design from a small prompt about building WCAG 2.2 compliant palettes for presentations matching my aesthetic preferences.
* For students: the only content generated by Claude here were the stylistic elements that, with the exception of the palettes, built upon a template for slide creation I had been using. No content, in the sense of language in the syllabus or the slides, was generated by Claude, that's all me. While I'm generally agnostic about whether Claude styles your presentations (it's a choice, but know it looks a way unless you bound it), Claude-generated material in terms of stuff you present me is never acceptable.
